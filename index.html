Shorts Uploader

Shorts Uploader is a personal tool, run by one person on their own computer, that uploads that person's own videos to their own YouTube channel and reads that channel's analytics.

It is not a public service. Nobody else can sign in to it, and it does not access anyone else's YouTube account or data.
